## 🐘 Charge to Victory: Elgin Community Rally

**Join the Team. Build the Momentum.**

The **Bastrop County Republican Party** invites all Elgin-area patriots, families, and conservative voters to our **Charge to Victory Community Rally** at the Elgin VFW!

Gather with local Republican leaders, hear from candidates, grab yard signs and campaign resources, and find out how you can help get out the vote in northern Bastrop County.

### Event Details

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Date** | Sunday, October 11, 2026 |
| **Time** | 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM |
| **Location** | Elgin VFW |
| **Address** | 118 Old Sayers Rd, Elgin, TX 78621 |
| **Admission** | FREE — Open to the Public |

### Why Attend?

- 🏛️ **Meet Candidates & Leaders** — Hear from conservative candidates and grassroots leaders.
- 📋 **Voter Resources & Yard Signs** — Get yard signs, voter guides, and precinct information.
- 🤝 **Grassroots Action** — Find out how you can join our block walking and poll coverage teams.
- 🇺🇸 **Community Fellowship** — Meet and unite with Elgin patriots working to keep Texas red.

**Join the Team. Build the Momentum. Charge to Victory!**
