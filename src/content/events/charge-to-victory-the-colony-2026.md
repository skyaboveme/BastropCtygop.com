## 🐘 Charge to Victory: The Colony Community Rally

**Join the Team. Build the Momentum.**

The **Bastrop County Republican Party** invites residents of The Colony and surrounding Bastrop communities to our **Charge to Victory Community Rally** at The Colony Amenity Center!

Meet conservative candidates, connect with your neighbors, pick up campaign signs, and learn how you can contribute to our countywide voter mobilization push leading up to Early Voting and Election Day.

### Event Details

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Date** | Sunday, October 11, 2026 |
| **Time** | 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM |
| **Location** | The Colony Amenity Center |
| **Address** | 368 Stephen F. Austin Blvd, Bastrop, TX 78602 |
| **Admission** | FREE — Open to the Public |

### Why Attend?

- 🏛️ **Candidate Engagement** — Meet and talk with conservative candidates running for local and statewide office.
- 📋 **Campaign Materials** — Grab yard signs, voter information, and sample ballots.
- 🤝 **Volunteer Opportunities** — Plug into precinct organizing, phone banks, and poll greeting efforts.
- 🇺🇸 **Neighborhood Community** — Stand with your neighbors in The Colony to protect conservative values in Bastrop County.

**Join the Team. Build the Momentum. Charge to Victory!**
