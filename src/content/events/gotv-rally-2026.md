## 🇺🇸 GOP Get Out the Vote Rally

**Vote Like Your Country Depends On It… Because It Does!**

The **Bastrop County Republican Party** and **Lost Pines Republican Women** invite you to the **GOP Get Out the Vote Rally** — a free, public event to register voters, celebrate Republican accomplishments, and energize conservative voters for Election Day.

### Event Details

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Date** | Sunday, October 4, 2026 |
| **Time** | 1:30 PM – 5:00 PM |
| **Location** | Bastrop Convention Center |
| **Address** | 1408 Chestnut Street, Bastrop, Texas 78602 |
| **Admission** | FREE — Open to the Public |

### What to Expect

- 🎵 **Live Band** — The Nite Shift
- 🏛️ **GOP Candidates** — Statewide, Judicial, and County candidates
- 🎤 **Emcee: Tim Young** — Comedian, Host, Speaker, Author, and Host of @TimRunsHisMouth

### Why Attend?

This is a rally to register voters, remind Republicans of our accomplishments over 18 months, and energize Republican voters for Election Day. Hear from candidates, enjoy live music, and leave fired up to elect our great candidates.
