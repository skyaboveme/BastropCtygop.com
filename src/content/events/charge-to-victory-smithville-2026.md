## 🐘 Charge to Victory: Smithville Community Rally

**Join the Team. Build the Momentum.**

The **Bastrop County Republican Party** invites all patriots, families, and conservative voters across Smithville and Bastrop County to our **Charge to Victory Community Rally**.

Come out to meet local candidates, connect with your precinct leaders, pick up campaign materials, and get involved as we organize to maximize voter turnout for the upcoming election!

- 🏛️ **Meet Candidates & Leaders** — Hear directly from Republican candidates and local grassroots leaders.
