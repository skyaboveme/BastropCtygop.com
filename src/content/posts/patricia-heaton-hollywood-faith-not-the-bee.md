## Patricia Heaton: 'How to Navigate Hollywood Without Losing Your Soul'

In a powerful interview on the *Not the Bee* podcast, actress Patricia Heaton — known for her roles on *Everybody Loves Raymond* and *The Middle* — opened up about the challenges and rewards of living out her Christian faith in one of America's most hostile environments for conservatives: Hollywood.

### A Faith-First Approach

Heaton, who has become one of the entertainment industry's most vocal conservative Christians, shared how her faith guided her throughout her career — even when it meant going against the grain of Hollywood's progressive orthodoxy.

> "You have to have a healthy fear of the Lord that shapes your response to criticism. The pressures of this industry are real, but they're nothing compared to the peace that comes from staying true to your values."

### Political Evolution

Heaton also discussed her evolving political views. In 2020, she publicly stated she was not a Trump supporter. By 2026, her position has shifted dramatically:

- She now says the country is **"on the right track"** under the Trump administration
- She credits the President's leadership on issues of faith, family, and national security
- She's been actively involved with the **October 7 Coalition** supporting Israel

### Standing Up to "Woke" Culture

When asked about navigating Hollywood's "woke" culture, Heaton offered practical advice rooted in humility and prayer rather than political combat — but made clear she won't be silenced:

*"I'm not interested in being liked by the right people. I'm interested in doing the right thing."*

### Why This Matters

Heaton's story is a reminder that conservative values aren't just for politicians — they're for everyone willing to stand up and be counted, whether in Washington, Austin, or right here in Bastrop County.
