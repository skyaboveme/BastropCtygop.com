## WHCA Dinner Returns Under Heightened Security After April Shooting

The rescheduled White House Correspondents' Association dinner took place Friday night at the Waldorf Astoria in Washington, D.C. — three months after a gunman disrupted the original event at the Washington Hilton, forcing the evacuation of President Trump and hundreds of attendees.

### What Happened in April

On April 25, 2026, 31-year-old Cole Tomas Allen opened fire near a security checkpoint at the Washington Hilton during the annual WHCA dinner. President Trump and other officials were immediately evacuated and the event was canceled. Allen has been charged with multiple federal felonies, including the attempted assassination of the President, and has pleaded not guilty.

### Friday's Event

Determined to show that violence would not silence the press or intimidate the nation's leaders, the WHCA board rescheduled the dinner under dramatically enhanced security measures:

- **New venue** — the Waldorf Astoria replaced the traditional Washington Hilton
- **No red carpet or cocktail reception** — to minimize exposure
- **Private screening** and a smaller, more intimate ballroom setting
- **President Trump attended and delivered a speech** — his first appearance at the annual dinner as president
