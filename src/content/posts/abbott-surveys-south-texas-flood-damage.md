## Governor Abbott Surveys South Texas Flood Damage, Praises Local Preparedness

Governor Greg Abbott conducted an aerial assessment of flood-devastated areas in South Texas on Friday, surveying the widespread damage caused by historic flooding along the Nueces River and surrounding communities.

### The Damage

Three flood-related deaths have been confirmed across the state after widespread flooding swept through the Hill Country and moved downstream:

- **Val Verde County:** A driver was swept away after attempting to cross high water
- **Uvalde and Comfort:** Two earlier deaths were reported from vehicles caught in flash floods
- **Nueces River communities:** The river reached major flood stages, inundating areas around Cotulla, Three Rivers, and surrounding counties including La Salle, Live Oak, and McMullen

### Abbott's Response

Governor Abbott praised local officials for their proactive response, noting that **no lives were lost** in La Salle, Dimmit, and McMullen counties thanks to effective advance preparation:

- Door-to-door notifications days before the river crested
- Pre-positioned evacuation resources
- Early warnings and road closures

The state has deployed personnel from **over 30 agencies**, along with high-water vehicles, boats, and aircraft for search, rescue, and recovery operations.

### The Takeaway

Governor Abbott reiterated the critical safety message: **"Turn Around, Don't Drown."** Texans should never attempt to drive through flooded roadways. His leadership during this crisis demonstrates the effectiveness of state-level emergency coordination — exactly the kind of responsive governance Texans expect.
