## One Year of the Big Beautiful Bill: What It Means for Texas Families

July 4, 2026 marked the **one-year anniversary** of President Trump signing the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA) into law — the cornerstone budget reconciliation package of his second term. As the dust settles, here's what the legislation has meant for families in Bastrop County and across Texas.

### Key Provisions

The OBBBA delivered on several major conservative priorities:

| Provision | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| **2017 Tax Rates Made Permanent** | Working families keep more of their paychecks — no more uncertainty about tax bracket sunsets |
| **Trump Accounts** | Every American newborn receives a savings account, investing in the next generation |
| **Energy Policy Reforms** | Streamlined permitting and expanded domestic energy production to reduce dependence on hostile foreign nations |
| **Spending Adjustments** | Targeted reforms to rein in government waste and redirect resources to priorities that matter |

### What It Means Locally

For Bastrop County families, the permanent tax rates alone provide **real, tangible relief**. No more worrying about whether Washington will let your taxes go up. The energy provisions support Texas' position as the nation's top energy producer, protecting jobs and keeping energy costs as low as possible — even amid the current Middle East tensions.

### The Conservative Vision

The OBBBA represents exactly what Republican governance looks like in practice: lower taxes, less regulation, more freedom, and investment in America's future. It's the kind of bold, decisive action that grassroots conservatives have been demanding for decades.
