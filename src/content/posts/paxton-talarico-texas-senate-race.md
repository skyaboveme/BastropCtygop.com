## Paxton Takes on Talarico: Texas Senate Race Heats Up for November

The 2026 Texas U.S. Senate general election is shaping up to be one of the most competitive — and consequential — races in the country. Republican nominee **Ken Paxton**, the Texas Attorney General, is facing Democrat **James Talarico** in a battle that will test the strength of conservative values in the Lone Star State.

### How We Got Here

Paxton secured the Republican nomination after defeating longtime incumbent Senator John Cornyn in the May 26 primary runoff — a decisive victory powered by a late endorsement from **President Donald Trump** and fueled by strong grassroots conservative support.

Talarico, a state representative from the Austin area, won the Democratic primary after defeating U.S. Representative Jasmine Crockett.

### The Campaign Trail

At a recent campaign event in Fort Worth, Paxton went on the offensive, accusing Talarico of being **hostile toward law enforcement** and out of step with Texas values. Paxton has built his campaign on his record of:

- **Fighting federal overreach** through aggressive legal challenges
- **Defending the border** and supporting Texas' right to secure its own territory
- **Protecting Second Amendment rights** and constitutional liberties

### The Stakes

Recent polling shows the race is highly competitive, with some surveys describing it as a statistical dead heat. Talarico's campaign announced a record-breaking $30 million fundraising quarter — more than tripling Paxton's $9 million — signaling that national Democrats are pouring resources into the race.

**This is exactly why every Republican vote in Bastrop County matters.** The November 3 general election will determine whether Texas sends a proven conservative fighter or a liberal Austin Democrat to the U.S. Senate.
