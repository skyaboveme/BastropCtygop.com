## Houthis Attack Saudi Oil Tankers as Red Sea Tensions Boil Over

The Iran-backed Houthi rebels in Yemen have dramatically expanded their attacks, targeting two Saudi-flagged oil tankers in the Red Sea and claiming a missile strike on the Saudi city of Jizan on Saturday.

### The Escalation

The Houthis announced a maritime blockade, threatening commercial shipping across one of the world's busiest trade routes. Saudi Arabia has responded with retaliatory military strikes against Houthi positions in Yemen, marking a dangerous new chapter in the regional conflict.

### Impact on Energy Markets

The combined effect of the Strait of Hormuz tensions and the Red Sea attacks has sent **global oil markets into turmoil**:

- **Brent crude surged above $100/barrel** — the highest level since the initial conflict began
- **U.S. gas prices are climbing** heading into the final weeks of summer
- Energy analysts warn that prolonged disruptions could push prices even higher through the fall

### What It Means for Texas

As the nation's top energy-producing state, Texas has a unique stake in these developments. While higher oil prices can benefit Texas producers, rising fuel costs impact every family filling up at the pump — including right here in Bastrop County.

President Trump's "energy dominance" strategy and the administration's push for increased domestic production are designed to insulate American families from exactly these kinds of global disruptions.
